Quality

PSNR on — held-out patches the model never trains on, measured with real rounding. Training PSNR is the noise-proxy figure and reads lower — that gap is expected, not a fault.

Rate

Bits per pixel straight out of the entropy model. lambda pins this: the run should hold a roughly flat rate while quality climbs. A rate that keeps falling is buying its numbers by throwing the picture away.

Rate–distortion loss

lambda × distortion + rate — the single number the optimiser actually minimises. The best checkpoint is chosen on the validation line, never the training one.
